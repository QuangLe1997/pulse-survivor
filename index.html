How to play

🕹️ Move with WASD / arrows, or drag your thumb on mobile. Your weapon auto-fires the nearest enemy.

💎 Kills drop glowing shards — grab them to fill your XP bar. On level-up, pick 1 of 3 upgrades.

⚡ Shards also charge the OVERDRIVE meter. When full, hit SPACE / the ⚡ button for a 5s burst: rapid fire + invincibility.

👹 A BOSS storms in around 90s. Survive as long as you can!
